  `developer_assistant_v1` registries — one realm/project/space, participants,
  typed contributions (`utterance|goal|question|claim|observation|evidence|
  proposal|critique|synthesis|result|decision_reference|system_notice`) and
  relations (`addresses|supports|challenges|refines|qualifies|supersedes|
